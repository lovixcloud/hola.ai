# hola.ai User Quickstart Guide

1. **Sign in to Your Workspace**: Log in with demo credentials `alex@acme.com` / `password123`.
2. **Upload Business Data**: Go to **Knowledge Base**, click **Add Knowledge Source**, and paste or upload your business product JSON or FAQ document.
3. **Customize Your Chatbot**: Go to **My Chatbots** and open the **Visual Builder**. Adjust widget brand colors, welcome greetings, and conversation starters.
4. **Publish & Embed**: Click **Publish Chatbot**, copy the generated `<script>` embed snippet, and paste it into your website.
